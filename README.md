# NovaOne

### A Contribution-Based Economic Protocol

NovaOne is a contribution-based protocol architecture designed to support participation, contribution recognition, ownership, governance, economic coordination, and long-term ecosystem evolution.

The project explores how meaningful contribution may be recognized through transparent and verifiable processes while preserving participant freedom, economic autonomy, and adaptability to future technological and societal change.

## Vision

NovaOne investigates the foundations of future economic coordination systems capable of supporting individuals, organizations, communities, and emerging technological infrastructures within an evolving ecosystem.

## Core Principles

* Contribution Recognition
* Participant Ownership
* Transparent Governance
* Economic Coordination
* Accountability
* Continuous Evolution
